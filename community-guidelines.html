Challenge Valley Community Guidelines

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Challenge Valley sends players out into the real world. These rules keep that fun and safe for everyone. They apply to everything you do in the app: challenges you create or play, posts, photos, profiles, usernames, stickers, invitations, and reports. You must be at least 13 years old to use Challenge Valley.

Play safely and legally

  • Obey local laws, posted signs, park rules, and closures. Your safety comes before any challenge.
  • Never trespass or enter private, restricted, or closed property.
  • Don’t play while driving. Pay attention to traffic, weather, terrain, and water.
  • Know your limits. Skip any challenge that feels unsafe and report it.

Create safe challenges

Challenges you create are reviewed before they appear on the map. Don’t create challenges that encourage or require:

  • trespassing, entering restricted areas, or breaking any law;
  • reckless driving, speeding, or playing while driving;
  • dangerous stunts or risky activity, such as climbing cliffs or structures, jumping from heights, or swimming in unsafe water;
  • weapons, fireworks, alcohol, drugs, or other substances;
  • damaging, defacing, or taking property, plants, or wildlife;
  • approaching, filming, or bothering strangers, or anything that could hurt someone.

Place challenge locations in public, accessible places.

Respect people’s privacy

  • Get permission before posting a photo where someone can be recognized.
  • Don’t share anyone’s personal information, such as their home address, phone number, school, or license plate.
  • Don’t use challenges or posts to track, follow, or locate someone.

Content that isn’t allowed

  • Sexual or sexually suggestive content, nudity, or anything that sexualizes minors
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others
  • Hate speech or content that attacks people for who they are
  • Graphic violence or gore
  • Impersonating another person, brand, or Challenge Valley
  • Spam, scams, advertising, or misleading content
  • Content you don’t have the right to share, including other people’s photos or copyrighted work

Play fair

  • Don’t reveal secret answers, code words, or hidden locations.
  • Submit only your own, real evidence. Don’t fake or edit photos, spoof your location, or use someone else’s results.
  • If you review completions of your challenge, review them honestly and consistently.
  • Don’t create extra accounts to get around a restriction or to boost your own points.

Reporting and blocking

If you see something that breaks these rules, report it from the challenge, post, or player profile. You can block any player at any time. See Support for step-by-step instructions. Moderators review reports and act on them as quickly as we can.

What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may:

  • reject a submitted challenge, completion, or post;
  • hide or remove content;
  • restrict an account, which limits posting publicly, creating challenges, adding friends, and sending invitations;
  • suspend an account temporarily;
  • permanently ban an account and delete it.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to suspension, a ban, or deletion without a warning first. We may also report illegal content or credible threats to the authorities.

Questions and appeals

If you think we made a mistake, email fedxemilio@gmail.com with your username and what happened. We’ll review it and may restore content or access.

We may update these guidelines as Challenge Valley changes. We’ll update the effective date above when we do.
