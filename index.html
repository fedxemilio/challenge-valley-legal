Challenge Valley Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Challenge Valley (“Challenge Valley,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a location-based adventure game for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Challenge Valley, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy.

Who we are

Challenge Valley is developed by Frederic Emilio. Privacy questions or requests can be sent to fedxemilio@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Challenge Valley is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of the app, and information collected automatically through device sensors you grant permission for.

Account information

  • Email address and password (used by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your password ourselves)
  • Username, display name, account creation date, and internal account identifiers

Profile information

  • Display name, self-description/bio, profile photo, featured sticker, and a broad home-town/origin label you choose to show on your profile
  • Profile stats: points, level, streaks, and completed/created/accepted challenge counts

Location information

  • Precise location, collected only during specific in-app actions — verifying you’re at a challenge location, completing a location- or photo-based requirement, and timing GPS-based time trials. We do not collect location in the background or while the app is closed.
  • A broad, self-reported home area you optionally set on your profile (used to center the map when live location isn’t available; this is a label you type in, not a GPS reading)

Photos and challenge content

  • Photos you choose to submit as evidence for a challenge requirement, using Apple’s built-in photo picker (we never gain access to your full photo library — only the specific images you select)
  • Challenge posts you create: captions, ratings, “vibe,” recommendation text, and any photos you choose to include
  • Challenges you author: titles, instructions, story text, hints, photos, coordinates, and requirement definitions
  • Comments, report details, and other free text you submit within the app

Social and gameplay data

  • Friendships, challenge invitations, and their statuses
  • Blocks you place on other users
  • Challenge progress, completion history, and completion reviews (including reviews from moderators or, for user-created challenges, the challenge’s creator)
  • Reports you file, and any resulting moderation record tied to your account

Device data stored only on your device

  • App preferences (haptics, sound, map appearance)
  • Draft challenges, draft posts, and personal notes you haven’t submitted yet
  • In-progress time-trial data, kept locally until a run finishes

We do not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not have in-app purchases. We do not access your contacts, camera roll (beyond photos you actively select), microphone, health data, or financial information.

How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • create and secure your account, and authenticate you;
  • operate core gameplay — showing nearby challenges, verifying GPS- and photo-based completions, and timing trials;
  • display your profile and content to other signed-in players, as described below;
  • power friendships, invitations, and social features;
  • review submitted challenges and completions, and respond to reports;
  • enforce our community rules, including suspending or banning accounts that violate them;
  • maintain, debug, and improve the app;
  • process account deletion requests.

Who can see your information

“Visible to other players” below means visible to other signed-in Challenge Valley users, not the public internet.

Visible to other signed-in players:

  • Your public profile: username, display name, bio, profile photo, broad home area, and profile stats
  • Challenges you’ve published
  • Posts you’ve marked public, including their photos, captions, ratings, and results

Visible only to you and our moderators (and, where noted, a relevant reviewer):

  • Your email address and full account record
  • Private posts and challenge drafts
  • Your challenge progress and completion reviews (submitted progress may also be seen by the creator of a user-created challenge you’re completing, for review purposes)
  • Friendships and invitations (visible to their participants)
  • Reports you file and any moderation record about your account

Never sold or shared for advertising. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Service providers

We use the following services to operate Challenge Valley. Each processes data on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms:

Our backend infrastructure runs on Google Cloud in the United States. If you use Challenge Valley from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Data retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so that features like history, friendships, and past challenges continue to work correctly. Content tied to community safety — reports, moderation records, and audit trails — may be retained even after related content or accounts are removed, to the extent needed to maintain platform integrity and meet legal obligations.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This requires re-entering your password. Once confirmed, we:

  • disable your login and remove local drafts and notes from your device;
  • delete your profile, stats, posts, progress, submissions, reviews, point history, invitations, blocks, and stickers;
  • delete your uploaded photos and media;
  • delete your account credentials.

Some limited, non-identifying records may be kept where needed to preserve the integrity of other users’ history (for example, a challenge you authored may remain visible to other players in an anonymized “Deleted challenge” form, and moderation records may be retained in redacted form). Content belonging to other users — their posts, progress, and completion history involving a challenge you created — is not deleted when you delete your account.

Deletion from our systems may take some time to complete, and copies may briefly persist in backups or logs before they age out.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: Most of your profile and content data can be viewed and edited directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Use in-app account deletion, or contact us.
  • Location: You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings; some gameplay features (like GPS-verified challenges) won’t work without it.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws like the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to request a copy of your data, request deletion, or object to certain processing. We do not sell or “share” (as defined under CCPA) personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address above.

Security

We use industry-standard measures — including Firebase Authentication and access-controlled cloud storage — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. We’ll update the effective date above when we do. Material changes will be reflected in an app update or in-app notice where appropriate.
