Challenge Valley Support

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Challenge Valley is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Contact us

Email fedxemilio@gmail.com for help with your account, a bug, a safety concern, or a question about a moderation decision. Include your username and, if it’s about specific content, the challenge or post name. We read every message and reply as soon as we can, but we can’t promise a specific response time.

In the app, you can also find these links under Profile → Settings (gear menu, top left) → Help & Legal.

Report content or a player

  • A challenge: open the challenge, tap the … menu, then Report Challenge.
  • A post: open the post and tap the report button (a speech bubble with an exclamation mark).
  • A player: open their profile, tap the … menu, then Report User.

Choose the reason that fits best and add details if you can. Our moderators review reports and may hide or remove content or restrict, suspend, or ban accounts. The person you report isn’t told who reported them.

Block a player

Open their profile, tap the … menu, then Block User. Blocking ends your friendship, cancels invitations between you, and hides their challenges and posts from you. Your own completed challenges and stats aren’t affected. To unblock someone, go to Profile → gear menu → Blocked Users. Unblocking doesn’t restore the friendship.

If a challenge seems unsafe

  • Don’t attempt it. No challenge is worth getting hurt, trespassing, or breaking the law.
  • Report it with the Unsafe Challenge reason so a moderator can review it.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

Delete your account

Go to Profile → gear menu → Settings → Delete Account and re-enter your password to confirm. Deletion is permanent. The Privacy Policy explains what is deleted and what limited records are kept. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account and we’ll help.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. If an account exists for that email, we’ll send a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn’t arrive.

Location problems

GPS challenges and time trials need your location while you use the app.

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app → Challenge Valley → Location.
  2. Choose While Using the App and turn on Precise Location.
  3. Try again outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Location is less accurate indoors and between tall buildings.

Challenge Valley never uses your location in the background or while the app is closed.

Photo problems

Challenge Valley uses Apple’s photo picker, so it only receives the photos you choose. It never sees the rest of your library, and there’s no photo-library permission to turn on. If a photo won’t upload, check your internet connection and try again. Your draft and selected photos are saved on your device until you submit.

Rules

Everyone must be at least 13 years old and follow the Community Guidelines.
